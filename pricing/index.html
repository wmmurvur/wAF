Straight answers

I already pay for Claude or ChatGPT. Why pay again?
Agent Farm never sells tokens. Your engines run on your own plans and keys, and that bill stays between you and them. What you pay for here is the farm around them: the relay, the night shift, the memory, the studio, the guards.
What happens when I reach a limit?
Nothing breaks and nothing is lost. A chain at its leg ceiling parks with its handover note, and a session over the limit waits in the queue. Carry it on by hand, or move up a plan.
Is there a trial?
The Free plan is the trial: every feature, with smaller limits, for as long as you like.
How does a yearly plan work?
A year costs 10 months, so two months are free.
Can I change plans or cancel?
Any time, from the customer page linked in your receipt. A cancelled plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
Can I get my money back?
Yes, within 14 days of your first payment, for any reason. The refund policy has the details.
Who takes the payment?
Lemon Squeezy, our reseller (merchant of record). It takes the payment, charges the VAT or sales tax of your country and sends the invoice, with your company's details if you add them.
What does a paid licence send to you?
It checks its key once a day: the key, a hash of the machine and the version. Nothing about your work. Without a connection it keeps working 30 days.
Will the prices change?
These are beta prices, and they may change before paid plans open. If you hold a plan, a new price applies only from your next renewal, and we tell you before it does.

The terms, the privacy policy and the refund policy say all of it in full.
