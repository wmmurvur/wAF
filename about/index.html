About

Built to keep going.
A farm of agents.

Agent Farm is made by MuVuSOFT. We wanted agents to keep working through a full context, an empty plan and the night, so we built the tool that lets them.

  • 1,053releases so far
  • 92things it does, in ten districts
  • 35,600+tests that pass before a release
  • 0runtime dependencies

Why it exists

Every AI company sells its own tool, and each one wants your work inside it. Each stops when a context fills up or a plan runs out, and each forgets what the last session learned.

Agent Farm sits in VS Code, beside the agent CLIs you already pay for, and makes them work as one farm: the work is handed on instead of stopped, every session is kept, and you can see what each step cost. It runs no model of its own.

How it is made

Agent Farm is built with Agent Farm. Agents in the farm work through its own backlog, hand the work on when a context fills, read its code and keep its technical notes up to date, and judges check what they bring back. A person decides what ships.

What we promise

  • Nothing about your work comes to us: no code, no prompts, no transcripts, no names of files or projects. There is no telemetry.
  • The free version never contacts us. A paid licence checks its key once a day, and keeps working for 30 days without a connection.
  • Your money back within 14 days of your first payment, for any reason.
