Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Agent Farm is built to run on your computer and keep your work there. This page says exactly what does reach us, why, for how long, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from anyone else.
  • The Free plan of Agent Farm never contacts us. There is no telemetry.
  • A paid licence checks its key once a day: the key, a hash of the computer and the version. Nothing else.
  • We never receive your code, prompts and answers, transcripts, file, folder and project names, account names, costs and tokens or which features you use, or how often.
  • If you reserve a paid plan, we keep your email address to write to you once, when paid plans open.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is MuVuSOFT ("we", "us"), the business name of Murat Vural, Vietnam. Write to agentfarm@muvusoft.uk about anything on this page.

Where we are based, Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 91/2025/QH15) applies. For people in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the GDPR and the UK GDPR apply as well. This policy is written to meet all of them; the legal bases below are named in the GDPR's terms.

2. This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. The fonts, photographs and scripts of agentfarm.space are served from the site itself, so opening a page sends no request to anyone else.

Server logs. Our web host, Hostinger, records each request to the site as every web server does: the IP address, the time, the page asked for, the browser's description of itself and the page you came from. These logs are kept for a short time to keep the site running and secure, and are not used to follow anyone. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

One note in your browser. The home page keeps one entry in your browser's session storage (af-boot) so that its opening animation plays once per tab. It never leaves your browser and is gone when you close the tab.

3. The early-access list

When you reserve a paid plan on the pricing page, we keep:

  • your email address;
  • the plan you chose and, if you said, what you would use Agent Farm for;
  • the time you signed up, and the version of this policy you agreed to.

We do not keep your IP address or anything about your browser with it.

Why: to write to you once, when paid plans open, about the plan you reserved. We do not add you to a newsletter and do not pass your address to anyone. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give with the box on the form and can take back at any time.

Where: in a file on our web host, outside the folders the website serves, and in a notification email to us. How long: until we have written to you after paid plans open, and no longer than six months after that; sooner if you ask. To be removed, write to agentfarm@muvusoft.uk or answer our email.

4. The Agent Farm extension

Agent Farm runs on your computer, inside Visual Studio Code. Your projects, prompts, transcripts and settings stay there.

  • The Free plan never contacts us. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting and no usage counting.
  • Your engines talk to their own providers (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) exactly as they do without Agent Farm, under those providers' privacy policies. We are not part of that connection.
  • Services you switch on, such as Telegram, GitHub, Airtable or a webhook, are reached directly from your computer, under their own privacy policies.
  • The phone app connects straight to your computer over an encrypted connection, after you pair it with a one-time code. Nothing passes through us.

5. A paid licence

A paid plan checks its key when you activate it and then once a day. Each check sends:

  • the licence key;
  • a hash of the identifier Visual Studio Code gives the computer: it tells one computer from another, not whose it is or what it is called;
  • the version of Agent Farm.

The answer says whether the key is valid and until when. We keep, for each key: its plan, which computer hashes are active on it and when each last checked in. That is what lets a key be used on the number of computers its plan allows.

It never sends your code, prompts and answers, transcripts, file, folder and project names, account names, costs and tokens or which features you use, or how often.

Legal basis: providing the plan you bought (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). How long: while the licence is active, and for 12 months after it ends, in case you come back or a question about it arises.

6. Buying a plan

Paid plans are sold by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. It collects your name, email address, billing address, payment details and, if you give it, your tax number, under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

From Lemon Squeezy we receive your name, email address, country, what you bought, and your licence key and its status. Why: to provide the plan, answer your questions and keep the records the law requires. Legal basis: the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)). How long: as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of a sale, and otherwise as for the licence above.

7. Writing to us

If you email us, we keep the conversation for as long as it takes to help you and to deal with anything that follows from it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, or the contract if you are a customer.

8. Who else handles data

WhoWhat for

They handle data only on our behalf or under their own policies as described above. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising. We disclose it to authorities only when the law requires it.

9. Data outside your country

The services above may handle data outside Vietnam and outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States. Where the law requires it, the transfer is protected with safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and recorded as the law of Vietnam asks.

10. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you how we use your personal data, to show it to you, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it, or to give it to you in a portable form, and you may object to our use of it. Where we rely on your consent, you may take it back at any time; that does not affect what was done before. Write to agentfarm@muvusoft.uk: we confirm we have your request within two working days and carry it out within 30 days at most.

You may also complain to a data protection authority: in Vietnam the Department of Cyber Security and High-Tech Crime Prevention (A05) of the Ministry of Public Security, in the United Kingdom the Information Commissioner's Office, in the European Union the authority of your country. We would like the chance to put things right first.

11. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection, the early-access list is kept outside the folders the website serves, and we keep as little as we can. The less there is, the less there is to lose.

12. Children

Agent Farm is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly keep their data. If you think a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

The date at the top says when this policy last changed. If we change what we collect or why, we say so on this page before it applies, and we email customers about any change that matters to them.
