Questions

Does Agent Farm make the images and videos itself?

No. It runs no model of its own: a media step calls the fal.ai or Replicate model you name, on your own key, and Agent Farm downloads, judges, files and assembles what comes back.

What does a production cost?

What your providers charge for the models you pick, and nothing more from us. Each job is priced by your rules and shown beside your text spend, a monthly media budget warns when you pass it, and the FFmpeg cuts on your machine cost nothing.

Do I need Adobe Premiere?

No. Premiere opens the XML directly, and the EDL beside it is a standard any editor reads. You can also just take the files from their stage folders.

Will it post to my channels without asking?

Not unless you tell it to. A publish waits for your yes by default, can be set to only rehearse, and goes out through your own upload flow; Agent Farm holds no platform password.

Can I use clips I made somewhere else?

Yes. Drop them in the waiting area to be looked at and filed, or in a watched folder to be registered where they are. They can be judged, kept, placed in a scene and exported like anything an agent made.

Is this a real part of the product or a side demo?

It is a real part. The whole chain, from a video split into parts, each edited and judged, to the parts joined over the original sound and filed as final, was run end to end with no copy and paste before it was called done.
